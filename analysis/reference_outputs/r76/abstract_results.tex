Without its programs the same pipeline reaches a geometric mean of 48.0$\times$ instead of 65.2$\times$, a hand-written renderer library does not recover the difference, and two further complete runs keep all four narrow wins over DeLog. On the twelve completely timed files, encoding runs at 12.9--19.5\% of DeLog's throughput (byte-weighted and geometric mean).
