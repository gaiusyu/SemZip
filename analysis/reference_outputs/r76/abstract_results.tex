Across sixteen LogHub logs and four web-server logs never used during development, with every archive required to decode byte for byte on its own, \tool\ produces the smallest archive on 18 of 20 logs (all except Calgary and the largest, Thunderbird, which dominates summed archive bytes) among DeLog, general-purpose compressors, and byte-exact adaptations of LogNexus, LogShrink and LogReducer, while encoding 3--8$\times$ more slowly than DeLog (byte-weighted). Without the synthesized programs, the same pipeline\textquoteright s geometric-mean compression ratio falls from 65.2$\times$ to 48.0$\times$. Most of this gain comes from separating rendered fields, and a smaller share from storing them as values.
