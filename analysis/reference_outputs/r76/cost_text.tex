Across the twelve files of session A, \tool's geometric-mean encoding and decoding throughputs are 19.5\% and 47.1\% of DeLog's, and it is slower than DeLog on every file in both directions. Geometric means favor small files, where process start-up dominates both tools; weighted by bytes, \tool\ encodes at 2.71 and decodes at 16.95~MB/s versus 21.09 and 45.80~MB/s for DeLog.
Construction is a one-time offline cost per log source: five syntheses, the gate, and selection (above) amortize over every later block, and archival storage is written once and restored rarely. For sources where it is smaller, \tool\ suits cold archives that tolerate a 5--8$\times$ slower encoder; for hot or near-line logs, DeLog's faster encoding is the better trade.
