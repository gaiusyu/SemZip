We also run the same pipeline on four public web-server access logs (Table~\ref{tab:unseen}), a format absent from the sixteen LogHub files. \tool\ is smaller than DeLog on 4/4 files and 4/4 suffixes, with 9.0--13.2\% higher ratios and 9.9\% fewer summed bytes. It is also smaller than LogLite-BL and every general-purpose setting on every file. The adapted baselines are closer. On Calgary, LogShrink+R and LogReducer+R are 0.5--0.6\% smaller than \tool, and on the 24 of USask's 25 blocks that LogShrink+R encodes, it is 1.3\% smaller. LogShrink+R fails on ClarkNet and USask, and LogReducer+R on USask. LogNexus+R, with its default threshold, is 4.4--20.3\% larger than \tool\ on all four.
