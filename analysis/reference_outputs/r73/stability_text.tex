Single syntheses vary substantially (Figure~\ref{fig:stability}). Across the five pooled syntheses, the largest single-synthesis archive exceeds the smallest by a median of 14.1\% per dataset and by up to 55.3\% (Zookeeper). Greedy decoding is not reproducible either: repeating the greedy synthesis changes the archive on 8/12 datasets, by up to 30.8\%. The per-synthesis gate narrows the median spread to 7.2\%. The selected deployment is no larger than every gated single synthesis on 12/16 datasets; on the other 4, the best gated single synthesis is smaller by 1.60\% (OpenStack), 0.69\% (Mac), 0.02\% (HPC), 0.02\% (Android). The median single synthesis is 4.8\% larger than the selected deployment. On complete files, gating the greedy synthesis alone, without any new model call, raises the sixteen-file mean from 88.84$\times$ to 93.08$\times$; selection across the five syntheses reaches 99.41$\times$.
