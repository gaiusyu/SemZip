Block-0 reductions largely carry over: relative to SemZip-1, the selected plan changes block-0 archives by a median of -4.2\% and complete-file archives by -4.9\% (Spearman $\rho=0.88$ across datasets). Transfer is uneven: BGL's block-0 reduction of 27.8\% becomes 22.4\% on the complete file, whereas Thunderbird's 3.3\% shrinks to 0.3\%: on 1,662 of its 2,112 later blocks the frozen program is not even 1\% smaller than the empty program (supplement). No complete file grows relative to SemZip-1.
